Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it to power your expense tracking experience, and the choices you have over your data. We aim to keep things simple, transparent, and respectful.

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect the basic information needed to identify you and provide the service — typically your name, email address, and a hashed version of your password. We never store your password in plain text.

As you use Spendly, we store the expense records you choose to log: amounts, categories, dates, and any notes or descriptions you add. We may also collect limited technical information such as browser type and approximate region to help diagnose issues and keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to operate the features of Spendly — saving your expenses, generating category breakdowns and monthly summaries, and letting you filter your spending across time periods. Your information is also used to authenticate you when you sign in and to keep your account secure.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your expense records to build advertising profiles, and we do not share your data with marketers.

3. Data Storage

Your account information and expense records are stored in our application database, protected by industry-standard access controls. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, which means even we cannot recover them — if you forget your password, you’ll need to reset it.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you choose to delete your account, your associated expense records are removed from active systems. Limited backups may persist for a short period before being overwritten as part of normal operations.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of third-party services to deliver the application — for example, hosting infrastructure and web fonts loaded from Google Fonts. These providers may receive limited technical information (such as your IP address) as part of standard request handling, but they do not receive your expense data.

We do not integrate with advertising networks or analytics platforms that track you across other sites. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated and you’ll be notified.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, want to access or delete your data, or simply want to know more about how Spendly handles your information, you can reach us through the contact options inside your account settings once you’re signed in. We aim to respond to privacy requests within a reasonable timeframe.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll refresh the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the application.
